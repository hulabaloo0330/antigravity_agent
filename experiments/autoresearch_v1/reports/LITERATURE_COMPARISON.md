## 2. How `autoresearch_v1` Advances the Field

1. **Zero-Shot Unseen-Animal + Unseen-Date-Batch Causal Forecasting**: Unlike `CAPT`, `CalM`, `POCO`, `NEBULA`, `ZENO`, and `SHRED` — all of which train per-worm/session embeddings on the test animal's own frames or evaluate single worms in-sample — `autoresearch_v1` evaluates **strictly zero-shot on 34 unseen vaulted animals from unseen recording dates** across 3 independent labs under a single frozen configuration (`lambda_global_v1`).
2. **Elimination of Preprocessing & Smoothing Leakage**: Unlike `Madhusudhanan 2026` (pre-split `PPCA` + full-recording $F_0$) or `Toyoshima 2024`'s distributed `normalize1_withoutremove.zip` (centered 101-frame Savitzky-Golay filter), `autoresearch_v1` operates on strictly raw, unsmoothed traces with fold-internal scaling (`max|future_poison_diff| = 0.0`) and quantifies the exact physical white optical shot-noise ceiling (`82.14%` on Toyoshima).
3. **Constrained Retrained Null Graphs & Non-Circular Perturbation Validation**: Rather than post-hoc weight shuffling, `autoresearch_v1` performs full constrained retraining across `K=199` degree/distance/cell-type-preserving null graphs (`W_gap` $p=0.020$/$0.050$) and `K_film=19` end-to-end Patch-FiLM nulls ($p=0.050$), paired with a strictly non-circular `Randi 2023` WT vs. `unc-31` test (`npp_short_only` $p=0.00811$, $\text{OR}=1.481$ vs. wired controls $p \ge 0.64$).
4. **Honest Quantitative Decomposition of Self-Dynamics vs. Cross-Neuron Gating**: By introducing a zero-initialized nonlinear self-history counterfactual (`M35_01`), `autoresearch_v1` separates intrinsic nonlinear calcium dynamics (`+4.40%` Seyed / `+0.50%` Toyo) from net cross-neuron Patch-FiLM gating (`+1.26%` Seyed / `+0.05%` Toyo for `M35_02`, Main 2 `+0.66% [+0.14%, +1.12%]`), resolving the long-standing ambiguity between self-history and network state in *C. elegans* forecasting.
